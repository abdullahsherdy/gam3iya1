---
description: Quiz me on my pull request before merge
argument-hint: [branch or PR number]
---

Read the diff of $ARGUMENTS (default: the current branch against main). Ask me, one at a time, to explain in my own words: what each change does, why this approach, and what I rejected. After my answers, say which parts I explained well, which are unclear, and whether the change is ready to merge. Do not explain it for me before I try.
